# Omit

Omit is a lightweight ad, tracker, and ISP-unblocker shield for Android, Smart TV, and Windows 10/11. It works out of the box with zero setup and near-zero battery drain.

## Download & Update

- **Official Website:** https://acwac.github.io/Omit/
- **Android & Smart TV:** [Download Omit.apk](https://acwac.github.io/Omit/downloads/Omit.apk)
- **Windows 10 / 11:** [Download Omit-Windows.exe](https://acwac.github.io/Omit/downloads/Omit-Windows.exe)

## Features

- **Blocks ads & trackers with near-zero battery use** — keeps normal video and download traffic in the OS kernel.
- **Opens ISP-blocked sites at full speed** — local anti-DPI unblocking while keeping your local IP for UPI and banking.
- **Never breaks Google Play Games or sign-ins** — permanent safe list for Google, Facebook, Apple logins, and cloud saves.
- **Catches background spy apps** — spots apps waking up while your screen is off and stops them immediately.
- **Cleans tracking links** — share messy URLs to Omit to strip tracking tags and read without popups.
