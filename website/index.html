Honest Benefits & Trade-offs

Does ISP Site Unblocker hide my IP address? No. Your real IP remains visible to websites, it cannot spoof your country for Geo-blocks (e.g., US-only Netflix), and raw non-CDN server IP blocks require an external relay VPN.
Why doesn't Omit drain battery like traditional VPNs? Omit never routes 0.0.0.0/0 normal traffic through userspace. Only DNS + Trap-IPs touch Omit; everything else flows directly through the OS kernel.
