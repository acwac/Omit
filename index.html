Questions, answered honestly

No marketing fog — here is exactly what Omit does and does not do.

Does Omit drain my battery?

No. Only DNS traffic is routed through Omit. Video, downloads, and games still travel over the OS kernel at full speed, so the daily battery cost stays around 1%.

Can Omit break Google sign-in or Play Games?

No. Login, OAuth, Play Games, and cloud-save hosts sit on a permanent safe list for every app, and Offline Lock only applies to apps you pick yourself.

Does it hide my IP address?

No. Omit is not a VPN relay — it unblocks ISP-level DNS and DPI blocks while keeping your real IP, so UPI, banking, and streaming never break. Country-locked catalogues still need a real VPN.

What runs on Windows?

The Windows app runs a small local DNS sinkhole with the same 200,000+ rule set, offers encrypted upstream DNS, and restores your adapter settings automatically when you exit or if it crashes.

Is there an account or subscription?

None. Omit is free, open source, works offline out of the box, and never asks you to sign in.
